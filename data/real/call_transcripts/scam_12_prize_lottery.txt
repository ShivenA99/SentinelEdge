Congratulations! You have been selected as a grand prize winner in the Publishers Clearing House national sweepstakes. Your winning ticket number is PCH-2024-7842 and you have won a cash prize of $850,000 plus a brand new Mercedes-Benz.

My name is Betty Owens and I am calling on behalf of Publishers Clearing House in partnership with the Consumer Protection Agency and the Federal Bureau of Investigation. The FBI has been appointed to oversee the disbursement of all sweepstakes prizes over $500,000 to ensure they are delivered safely and securely to the winners.

Your prize package has already been prepared and is ready for delivery. However, before we can release your winnings, there are some mandatory fees that must be paid. Federal law requires all prize winners to pay a processing and insurance fee to cover the secure transport of the funds.

The total fee is $4,500 which covers the federal taxes, insurance, and courier fees. Now I know that might sound like a lot, but consider that you're receiving $850,000 plus a luxury vehicle. This fee represents less than one percent of your total winnings.

We need to process this fee today because the prize delivery is scheduled for tomorrow morning. A armored courier will bring the check directly to your home along with the keys to your new vehicle.

The most efficient way to pay the processing fee is with Green Dot MoneyPak cards or iTunes gift cards. You can purchase these at any Walmart, Target, or CVS. Once you have the cards, just call me back with the numbers and your prize will be on its way.

I assure you this is completely legitimate. I can give you my FBI badge number and you can verify it. This is a once in a lifetime opportunity and we would hate for you to miss out on your prize. Can you get to a store within the next hour to purchase the cards?
