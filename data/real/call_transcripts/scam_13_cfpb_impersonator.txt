# Notes: Reconstructed from CFPB's own documentation of scammers impersonating
#   CFPB employees. The CFPB reported scammers contact victims by phone and
#   video call claiming they can participate in a class-action lawsuit.

Good afternoon. My name is Robert Davis and I am a compliance officer with the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau, the federal government agency. We are reaching out to you because our records indicate that you may be eligible for a significant payout from a class-action settlement against several major banks.

This lawsuit was filed on behalf of consumers who were charged excessive overdraft fees and hidden service charges between 2018 and 2023. The settlement fund totals over $340 million and individual payouts range from $3,500 to $15,000 depending on the number of affected accounts.

I am calling to inform you that based on your banking history, you have been pre-approved for a settlement payment of approximately $8,200. This money is rightfully yours and we want to make sure you receive it.

Now, in order to process your claim and release the funds, there are some standard administrative requirements. First, I need to verify your identity by confirming your full name, current address, date of birth, and Social Security number. This is required under federal regulations to prevent fraud in the disbursement process.

Second, there is a mandatory tax withholding and processing fee of $450 that must be paid before the settlement funds can be released. This covers the IRS reporting requirements and the administrative costs of distributing the settlement. This fee will be deducted from your payout, but it needs to be paid upfront to initiate the disbursement.

We can accept payment via wire transfer, Zelle, or prepaid card. Once the fee is received, your settlement check of $8,200 will be mailed to your address within 5 to 7 business days. The deadline to claim your settlement is next Friday, so I encourage you to act on this today. Would you like to proceed with claiming your settlement funds?
